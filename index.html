The Pramodan Philosophy

Rooted in Purity.
Raised for Today.

Born from the fertile lotus ponds of Bihar, Pramodan takes India's ancient superfood—phool makhana—and reimagines it for modern, conscious snackers.

Unlike ordinary fried chips loaded with palm oil and chemical powders, every single foxnut at Pramodan is slowly dry-roasted in small batches to preserve its natural crunch, vitamins, and minerals.
