PRAMODAN — BRAND CASE STUDY

01 — HERO

Bringing Healthy Snacking Into a More Joyful Future.

How strategic branding transformed an everyday healthy snack into a
vibrant lifestyle brand designed to compete with conventional snack
foods.

02 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

Industry - Healthy Food - FMCG - Packaged Snacks

Services - Brand Strategy - Brand Identity - Logo Design - Packaging
Design - Product Architecture - Visual Identity - Color System -
Typography - Art Direction

Deliverables - Brand Strategy - Logo - Visual Identity - Packaging -
Product Architecture - Social Media Direction

03 — THE CHALLENGE

Pramodan was never intended to be only a makhana brand.

The founders wanted to build a healthy snacking brand capable of
replacing conventional unhealthy snacks such as chips with better
alternatives.

Makhana became the launch product, but the long-term vision included
honey, cookies, and future healthy food categories.

The challenge was creating a brand exciting enough to compete
emotionally with junk food while remaining authentic to healthy eating.

04 — DISCOVERY

Most healthy snack brands communicate nutrition well but fail to
communicate excitement.

Traditional snack brands win because they feel energetic, colourful and
emotionally engaging.

Rather than making healthy snacks look healthier, the opportunity was to
make healthy snacks feel just as desirable.

05 — STRATEGIC INSIGHT

Consumers rarely choose snacks based only on nutrition.

They choose products that create an emotional connection.

The strategy therefore focused on making healthy snacking feel joyful,
approachable and modern.

06 — BRAND STRATEGY

Pramodan was positioned as a healthy snacking brand instead of a
single-product brand.

The identity was designed to support future expansion into multiple food
categories while maintaining one recognizable master brand.

07 — LOGO STRATEGY

The brand name “Pramodan” is one of the many names of Lord Vishnu.

Rather than using direct religious imagery, the identity uses subtle
cultural symbolism.

The “O” in the wordmark is replaced with an illustrated makhana that
also resembles the Sudarshan Chakra.

This creates a memorable visual story while remaining contemporary and
approachable.

08 — BRAND PERSONALITY

The emotional inspiration comes from Lord Krishna’s joyful nature.

Instead of expressing religion visually, the identity expresses:

-   Joy
-   Playfulness
-   Warmth
-   Celebration
-   Positivity

09 — TARGET AUDIENCE

Rather than targeting only health-conscious consumers, the identity was
designed for a broad audience.

Bold, playful and minimal visuals make the brand approachable without
becoming childish.

10 — VISUAL IDENTITY

The visual language balances:

-   Boldness
-   Playfulness
-   Minimalism

Bright colours, expressive typography and clean layouts create a
memorable shelf presence.

11 — COLOUR SYSTEM

The master brand maintains a consistent identity while every product
category and flavour receives its own distinctive colour palette.

This creates shelf recognition while supporting future expansion.

13 — PACKAGING

Packaging combines bold colour, playful graphics and clear information
hierarchy.

The system is scalable across future product categories.

14 — OUTCOME

Pramodan evolved from a healthy snack concept into a scalable lifestyle
brand.

The project demonstrates how strategic positioning, cultural
storytelling and a flexible visual identity can transform a functional
food product into a memorable consumer brand.

15 — KEY TAKEAWAY

Healthy products should not only communicate nutrition.

They should communicate joy.

By shifting the focus from health alone to the experience of healthy
snacking, Pramodan became a brand capable of competing emotionally with
conventional snack brands while remaining authentic to its purpose.
